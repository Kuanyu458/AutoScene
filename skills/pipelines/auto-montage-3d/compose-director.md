# Compose Director — Auto Montage 3D

Read the FFmpeg and Remotion Layer 3 skills. Route strictly by the approved
`render_runtime` and composition mode; runtime/GL failures are blockers and
may not trigger a silent substitute. If HyperFrames was rejected as unavailable
in proposal, preserve that recorded constraint rather than silently invoking it
or claiming parity. Feed only the centralized TimelineV2 adapter into the
runtime. Mix the selected music window as AAC without altering edit timing.

Verify with ffprobe: 1920x1080, 30fps, H.264, AAC, audio present, and duration
within one frame. Sample each source/3D boundary and all 3D cue frames. Measure
planner and rendered cut-to-anchor errors, A/V drift, black frames, missing
assets, and expected versus rendered 3D insert count. The targets are planner
error at most one frame, rendered p95 at most two frames, and A/V drift at most
100ms. Write these results to both `render_report` and `final_review`; any
critical failure prevents a success claim.
